# Code of conduct

Hatchlybox follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else for Hatchlybox, is welcome whatever their
experience, background or identity, and treats everyone else with respect.

In short:

- Be kind, patient and constructive. Many people here don't code: explain without talking down.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexual or discriminatory language or images.
- Don't publish anyone's private information.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.

**Reporting.** If someone breaks these rules, tell the maintainers privately: on
[HatchlyBox/hatchlybox](https://github.com/HatchlyBox/hatchlybox), open the **Security** tab, then **Report a
vulnerability**, and start the title with "Code of conduct". Only the maintainers see it. They'll look into every
report, keep the reporter's identity private, and may warn, remove content, or ban someone from the project.
